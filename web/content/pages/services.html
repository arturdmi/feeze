Services

The development team at Tokiwa Software GmbH is working hard to make feeze a great tool that is freely available to the community.

We are using this tools mainly for our own performance analysis and optimizations, so the development is driven by our needs.

If you have specific requirements, feel free to contact us for services related to the feeze tool, in particular:

  • Analysis of scheduler-related performance of your application

  • Additional functionality for feeze such as

    • support for additional trace points
    • specific timing analysis like jitter or deadline misses
    • application-specific extensions
    • any idea or wish you might have

  • Support for specific Linux versions

  • Porting to specific (real-time) OSes.

  • etc.

You can reach us via info@tokiwa.software.
